About our society

Care that reaches people where they need it most.

Established in 2017 under the Sudanese Aid Commission Act, the Sudanese Green Crescent Society is a national non-profit organization committed to protecting health and preventing harm.

We support vulnerable groups—especially children, youth, mothers, and students—through health education and prevention programs addressing tobacco, drugs, alcohol, and other harmful substances.
